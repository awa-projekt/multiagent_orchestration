# Default Risk Matrix

A generic starting point. When the organization defines its own scales, bands, or risk
appetite, use those instead and say which matrix you applied.

## Likelihood (over the assessment horizon)

| Score | Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rare | Could happen only in exceptional circumstances (under 5%) |
| 2 | Unlikely | Not expected, but possible (5-25%) |
| 3 | Possible | Might happen; has happened elsewhere (25-50%) |
| 4 | Likely | Will probably happen; has happened here before (50-80%) |
| 5 | Almost certain | Expected to happen (over 80%) |

## Impact (worst credible outcome)

| Score | Level | Money | Operations | People and compliance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Minimal | Negligible | No noticeable disruption | No injury; no breach |
| 2 | Minor | Small, within team budget | Hours of disruption | First aid; minor internal policy breach |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable at department level | Days of disruption; some customers affected | Medical treatment; reportable minor breach |
| 4 | Major | Significant at company level | Weeks of disruption; many customers affected | Serious injury; regulatory breach; press coverage |
| 5 | Severe | Threatens objectives or viability | Critical process stopped long-term | Fatality; major regulatory action; lasting reputational harm |

Use the highest applicable column.

## Score bands (likelihood x impact)

| Score | Band | Default handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-4 | Low | Accept or monitor; review yearly |
| 5-9 | Medium | Treat where cost-effective; owner reviews quarterly |
| 10-16 | High | Treatment plan required; senior owner; review monthly |
| 20-25 | Critical | Escalate immediately to leadership; act before proceeding |

## Control effectiveness

| Rating | Meaning | Effect on residual score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effective | Designed well and evidenced as operating (tested, logged) | May lower likelihood or impact |
| Partly effective | Exists but gaps or no recent evidence | Lower by at most one step |
| Not effective or untested | On paper only | No reduction |
