# Business Document Skeleton

A default structure for business reports. Delete sections that do not apply rather than
filling them with placeholders; keep the order.

```markdown
# <Title>: <period>

Prepared for: <audience> | Prepared by: <author or team> | Date: <date>
Status: <final | draft | preliminary figures>

## Executive summary

- <Headline result in one sentence, with the key number and comparison.>
- <Most important change or driver.>
- <Main risk or issue, and its likely effect.>
- <Decision or action needed, from whom, by when.>

## Key figures

| Metric | This period | Comparison (<previous / target / budget>) | Change | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <metric> | <value, unit> | <value, unit> | <+/- absolute, %> | <system or document> |

## <Topic 1>

<Main point in one sentence.> <Evidence: figures, examples, with sources.>
<Interpretation, clearly marked as such.>

## <Topic 2>

<Same pattern.>

## Risks and issues

| Risk or issue | Impact | Likelihood or status | Owner | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Recommendations and next steps

1. <Action> - owner: <name> - due: <date> - expected effect: <effect>

## Data and method notes

- Sources: <systems, files, extraction dates>.
- Definitions: <how each metric is calculated>.
- Exclusions and adjustments: <what was left out or corrected, and why>.
- Known gaps: <missing or preliminary data, and when it will be complete>.

## Appendix

<Supporting tables and detail that the main text refers to.>
```

## Variants

- **Status update** (project or program): replace Key figures with a milestone table
  (milestone, planned date, forecast date, status) and add a short "since last period"
  section.
- **Incident or review**: after the summary, add Timeline (what happened when), Impact,
  Cause, and Corrective actions with owners. Keep it blameless and factual.
- **Recurring performance update**: keep the same metrics, definitions, and order every
  period so readers can compare; note any change in definition prominently.
