# Vendor Comparison Matrix

A generic template. When the organization prescribes its own evaluation form, criteria,
or weights, use that instead and say which one you applied.

## Must-haves (pass or fail)

| Must-have | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meets mandatory scope | pass | pass | pass |
| Required certifications or compliance | pass | pass | pass |
| Data location or security requirement | pass | pass | fail |
| Accepts standard contract terms or acceptable deviations | pass | pass | pass |

## Scored criteria (weights agreed before opening offers, total 100)

| Criterion | Weight | Vendor A | Vendor B | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total cost of ownership | 35 | 3 | 4 | cost sheet, section 2 |
| Functional or service fit | 25 | 5 | 4 | offer section 3, demo notes |
| Delivery and implementation | 15 | 4 | 3 | project plan, lead times |
| Support and service levels | 15 | 4 | 2 | SLA annex |
| Supplier risk and stability | 10 | 4 | 4 | financial check, references |
| Weighted score (out of 5) | 100 | 3.90 | 3.55 | sum(weight x score) / 100 |

## Score anchors

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Exceeds the requirement with clear evidence |
| 4 | Fully meets the requirement with evidence |
| 3 | Meets most of it; minor gaps with a workable mitigation |
| 2 | Significant gaps or weak evidence |
| 1 | Largely fails the requirement |
| 0 | Not addressed or no information |

Cost score: 5 for the lowest total cost; others proportionally, for example
`5 x lowest / this vendor's cost`, rounded to one decimal.

## Total cost of ownership items

- Purchase price or recurring fees over the full comparison period.
- One-time costs: setup, implementation, migration, training, delivery.
- Running costs: support, maintenance, consumables, internal effort.
- Exit costs: termination fees, data export, transition.
- Price escalation clauses and currency exposure.

## Checks before recommending

- Every score has an evidence reference.
- All vendors were scored by the same evaluators on the same information.
- Conflicts of interest were declared and handled.
- Sensitivity was tested on the heaviest weight.
