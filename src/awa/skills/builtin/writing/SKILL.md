# Writing

- Before writing, identify the reader, the purpose, and every explicit constraint
  (format, length, language, tone, required sections). Follow them exactly.
- Lead with the answer or main point; put supporting detail after it.
- Back every non-trivial factual statement with its source when sources are available.
  Never invent sources, quotations, figures, or citations.
- State uncertainty and gaps plainly instead of covering them with vague wording.
- Prefer plain, specific words, short sentences, one idea per paragraph, and consistent
  terminology. Cut filler and repetition.
- Use headings, lists, and tables when they help the reader scan, not by default.
- When revising, address every requested change explicitly, keep what was not flagged,
  and add no new unsupported content. Summarize what changed when asked.
- Reread the final text against the constraints before returning it.

## Before you return it

```text
Reader: <who> | Purpose: <what they should do or know>
Constraints: <format, length, language, tone> - met: yes
Lead: <the answer in the first sentence>
Sources: <claim - source>, none invented
Gaps stated: <what is unknown>
```
