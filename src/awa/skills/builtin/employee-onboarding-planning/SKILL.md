# Employee Onboarding Planning

A good onboarding makes the first day work and the first months productive. Every task
needs an owner and a due date relative to the start date, or it will not happen.

## Inputs

Collect: role and team, manager, start date, location or remote setup, employment type,
working hours, required equipment and systems, mandatory training for the role and
region, and any adjustments the new hire has asked for. Use only personal data the
plan needs.

## Plan by phase

### Before day 1 (from offer acceptance)

- Contract and required forms complete; payroll and benefits set up by HR.
- Accounts and access requested by the manager, following least privilege: only the
  systems the role needs now, with the approvals policy requires.
- Equipment ordered and shipped or set up; workspace or remote kit ready.
- Welcome message with start time, place or link, and who to ask for; buddy assigned.

### Week 1

- Day 1: welcome, equipment and login check, team introductions, the week's plan.
- Mandatory training (security, safety, data protection, conduct) scheduled with dates.
- Meetings with key contacts; a first small, real task with a clear finish.

### Days 1-90

- 30 days: knows the tools, processes, and people; completes first tasks with support.
- 60 days: owns regular work; contributes to the team's goals.
- 90 days: works independently on the role's core responsibilities.
Write goals specific to the role, agreed with the manager, and schedule check-ins
(end of week 1, day 30, day 60, day 90 or end of probation).

## Rules

- Every task has an owner (HR, manager, IT, facilities, buddy) and a due date relative
  to the start (D-10, D1, D+30).
- Access beyond the role's baseline needs a separate request and approval.
- Track completion; escalate overdue pre-start tasks at least three days before day 1.
- Collect feedback from the new hire at 30 and 90 days and improve the template.

## Plan format

```text
New hire: <role>, <team> | Start: <date> | Manager: <role> | Buddy: <role>
When  | task                                  | owner      | status
D-10  | Request accounts: email, ticketing    | manager    | open
D-5   | Laptop shipped, tracking sent         | IT         | open
D1    | Welcome, login check, team intro      | manager    | planned
D1-5  | Security and data protection training | new hire   | planned
D+30  | Check-in: goals 30 reviewed           | manager    | planned
Goals: 30 <...> | 60 <...> | 90 <...>
```

## Common failure modes

- Day 1 without a working login or equipment.
- Copying a colleague's full access instead of the role baseline.
- No goals, so probation ends without a shared view of performance.
