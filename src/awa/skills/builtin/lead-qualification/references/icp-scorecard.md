# Default ICP Scorecard

A generic starting point. When the organization defines its own ideal customer profile,
point values, or tier thresholds, use those instead and say which scorecard you applied.

## Fit criteria (1 point each when met, 0.5 when partly met)

| Criterion | Met when |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | The company operates in a target industry listed in the ICP. |
| Size | Headcount or revenue falls inside the target range. |
| Region | The company buys in a market the organization serves and can contract in. |
| Use case | The stated or evident need matches a problem the offering solves well. |
| Technical match | Required integrations, data location, or deployment constraints are supported. |

Fit: 4-5 high, 2-3.5 medium, below 2 low.

## Intent signals (1 point each when observed)

| Signal | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit request | Asked for a demo, trial, quote, or call. |
| Timeline | Stated a decision or start date within the next two quarters. |
| Authority | A budget holder or decision maker is involved in the conversation. |
| Active evaluation | Mentioned comparing options, a tender, or a replacement project. |

Intent: 3-4 high, 2 medium, 0-1 low.

## Tiers

| Fit \ Intent | High | Medium | Low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | A | A | B |
| Medium | B | B | C |
| Low | C | D | D |

## Hard disqualifiers

- The company or region is excluded by sanctions, export rules, or company policy.
- The market or language is not supported.
- The contact is a competitor, a student project, a job applicant, or a vendor pitching.
- The account is already owned by another team (route to that owner instead).

## Discovery questions for unknowns

- What prompted you to look at this now?
- How do you handle this today, and what is not working?
- Who else is involved in choosing and approving a solution?
- Is there a date by which you need this in place?
