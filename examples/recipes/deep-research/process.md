# Deep research

Someone asks a research question with several parts, for example: "Python, Java and Go: the year each first appeared and who designed it". Split the question into up to four aspects and research them on the open web at the same time.

Every finding is a claim with the address of the page and a word-for-word quote. Write a draft from the evidence only. If an aspect is missing or thin, research it again, at most two extra rounds.

Finally, add numbered citations: a claim is cited only when a checked quote supports it; a claim without evidence is removed and listed as unsupported. The report is saved only after a person approves. The answer lists the report, the references, unsupported claims, open questions and where it was saved.
