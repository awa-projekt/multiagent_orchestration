# Contract review

Before a contract is signed, its owner asks for a review. Take the contract and the record of the other party from our repository and prepare a fact sheet.

Review the contract from three sides at the same time, each against its own playbook: legal (liability, indemnity, termination, governing law), compliance (data protection, data location, sub-processors, audit rights) and commercial (payment terms, price changes, renewal, contract value). For every gap, quote the fallback wording from the playbook.

Merge the three reviews into one verdict: sign, sign with changes (every issue has a standard fallback), or escalate to legal (a position outside the playbook), with the findings ordered by priority. Nothing is signed or sent; the verdict goes to the contract owner.
