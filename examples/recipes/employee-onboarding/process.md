# Employee onboarding

When HR marks a new hire as signed, plan the whole onboarding from the hire's record, in the right order. IT creates the accounts and raises the purchase request for the hardware. HR creates the payroll record (employees only) and assigns a buddy. Facilities reserves a desk and then issues the badge (office-based hires only).

Once the accounts, the buddy and the desk exist, HR schedules the first week. Each team picks up its tasks as soon as they are ready and reports back what it created.

At the end, the hiring manager gets a status summary with a checklist that also lists what is still waiting for people, such as purchasing's approval of the hardware and the payroll administrator's approval.
