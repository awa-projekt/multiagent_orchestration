# Market research report

A product or strategy team asks a market question, for example "predictive maintenance software for mid-sized manufacturers in the DACH region". Plan the study: market research and competitor research run in parallel.

An analyst turns the findings and the market statistics into figures and a clearly labeled projection, and a writer drafts the report. A reviewer checks each claim against its source; an unsourced or overstated claim is revised once and checked again.

The approved report, with its list of sources, is saved to our knowledge base and returned. Missing data is reported as a gap, never estimated.
