# Lead qualification

Inbound leads land in our CRM from demo requests, webinars and the newsletter. Existing customers go straight to their account manager.

For prospects, research the company (key facts and news from the last six months) and score it against our ideal customer profile, criterion by criterion, with evidence. Facts the research cannot find are marked unknown for manual research, never estimated. Write the score, the tier and a summary back to the CRM, whose own rules then start nurture campaigns or sales follow-up.

A and B leads get a short, personal first email that refers to something concrete about their company. A person reads and approves every email before it is sent.
