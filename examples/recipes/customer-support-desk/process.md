# Customer support desk

Every customer message first goes to a first-line agent. It identifies the customer, recalls what we already know about them and answers how-to questions straight from our knowledge base.

Billing, technical and order or return questions are handed to a specialist who keeps the conversation until the topic changes. Duplicate charges and damaged items are refunded on the spot; refunds above 50.00 wait for a support lead's approval.

Customers who ask for a person, mention legal steps or keep coming back with the same problem are handed to our human queue with a summary. Lasting facts such as preferences, refunds and promises are remembered for the next conversation.
