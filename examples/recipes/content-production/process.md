# Content production

Marketing files content requests: a blog post, a social media post or a newsletter item. Turn each request into a brief with audience, key message, keywords, length and call to action.

Write a draft from the brief and our approved product facts only. An editor checks the draft against the brief and our brand guidelines and sends it back with numbered feedback until it is approved, at most three rounds.

A compliance review then checks the approved text against our marketing rules: no guarantees, figures with their basis, the mandatory disclaimer, and customer names only with consent. Compliant content is saved in the content system as ready for publication and published once the content owner approves.
