# Purchase requisition

Employees send purchase requisitions by email: what is needed, roughly what it costs and which cost center pays. Read the requisition, pick an approved supplier from our supplier list and check the cost center's budget.

Then issue the purchase order and draft the order email to the supplier. Orders above 1,000 EUR wait for the budget owner's approval before they are issued, and every email to a supplier is released by a person.

A requisition that is incomplete, over budget or names a supplier that is not approved is declined or sent back, with the rule of our purchasing policy that applies.
