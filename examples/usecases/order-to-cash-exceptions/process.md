# Order-to-cash exceptions

Our orders, invoices and payments live in three places: the order database, the invoice workbook (Excel) and the bank payments (CSV). For a customer or an order, compare each order with its invoices and payments.

Report every exception with the action to take: shipped but not invoiced, invoiced more than shipped, a duplicate invoice, overpaid, a small short payment to write off, underpaid, or overdue.

For every invoice that is really open and overdue, prepare a dunning letter (level 1, 2 or 3 by the days it is late). A person approves each letter before it is sent. While a billing error is open, the customer is not chased; the invoice is corrected first.
