# Recruiting CV screening

Applications for an open role arrive as CVs in PDF or Word. Read each CV and turn it into a job-related profile: skills, years of professional experience, languages and email address.

Score every profile against the role profile, criterion by criterion, with points and a reason for each, and rank the candidates. Only job-related criteria count.

Candidates at or above the shortlist limit who meet every required criterion are invited to the next free interview slots. A recruiter approves each invitation before it is sent. The answer explains every score with the role's criteria.
