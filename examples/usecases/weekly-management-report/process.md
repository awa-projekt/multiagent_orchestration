# Weekly management report

Every Monday morning management gets a short report on the last complete week (Monday to Sunday). Take revenue, number of orders, gross margin and new customers from our sales database for that week and the week before, and compare them with the budget.

Explain every notable variance from the data, with breakdowns by region and product and their amounts; when the data does not explain a variance, say so. Also report data problems, such as orders without a region.

Write it as a short email with a headline, a table of the figures and one paragraph per variance. The email is sent only after a person approves it.
