# Supplier due diligence

Before a new supplier is onboarded, compliance wants three checks: is the company or one of its owners on a sanctions list, is there adverse news (fraud, corruption, serious proceedings), and who owns the company.

Read the candidate's row in the onboarding list and our checklist, research each check on the web, and report per check a result (clear, concern, hit or no data), one sentence and the address of the page it is based on.

Score the risk with fixed rules: a sanctions hit is critical, a concern is high, a missing or unsourced check is medium, otherwise the risk is low. The decision is recorded only after a compliance officer approves it. The answer is a risk summary with the sources and the open items.
