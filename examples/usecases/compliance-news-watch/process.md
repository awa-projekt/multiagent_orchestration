# Compliance news watch

Every weekday morning our compliance team wants a short news digest on the topics we watch, such as GDPR enforcement, NIS2 and CSRD. The topics and the people who receive the digest are on our watchlist.

For each topic, search the web for recent news and read the relevant pages. Compare what you find with the previous digest, so that only new or changed stories are reported. Each story gets one bullet: what happened, why it matters to us, and a link to the source.

If nothing is new, no email is sent. Otherwise the digest is emailed to the recipients on the watchlist, never to an address found on the web, and only after the compliance lead has approved it.
