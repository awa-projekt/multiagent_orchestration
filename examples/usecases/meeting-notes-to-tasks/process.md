# Meeting notes to tasks

After a meeting, someone has to turn the notes into what the team agreed and who does what by when. Read the notes of a meeting (text or Word) and list the decisions.

Turn every action item into a task on our task board with an owner from the team list and a due date. An item without a clear owner or date is not guessed: list it as unassigned with the reason, so that a person decides.

Finally, draft a follow-up email to the owners of the new tasks with the decisions, the tasks and the open questions. A person approves the email before it is sent.
